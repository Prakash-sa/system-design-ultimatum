# Code of Conduct

This project is a learning resource. Contributors and maintainers are expected to keep discussions professional, respectful, and focused on improving the repository.

## Expected Behavior

- Be constructive and specific in feedback.
- Assume good intent while still asking for evidence or clarification when needed.
- Respect different levels of experience.
- Keep pull request and issue discussions on topic.
- Give credit for ideas, references, and source material.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Repeated off-topic comments after maintainers ask for the discussion to move or stop.
- Submitting content you do not have the right to contribute.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues or pull requests; or block contributors whose behavior harms the project. For sensitive conduct concerns, contact the maintainer privately when possible.
